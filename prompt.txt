Refine the SabiLabs website visual system so the LIGHT / WHITE theme follows the official brand more closely.

Use this primary light-theme palette:

WHITE + BLACK/CHARCOAL + SABI GREEN

Do NOT use blue as the main accent in the white theme.

The official SabiLabs identity should remain the visual anchor.

==================================================
1. LIGHT THEME COLOR SYSTEM
==================================================

Use:

Background:
#FFFFFF

Secondary background / section surface:
#F7F9F7

Primary text:
charcoal / near-black
approximately:
#151A17

Secondary text:
muted grey-green
approximately:
#66706A

Primary brand accent:
SabiLabs green

Borders:
very light grey-green
approximately:
#E2E8E4

Hover surfaces:
soft off-white / pale green-grey

Do not use random cyan, gold, orange, purple, or unrelated accent colors in the white theme.

==================================================
2. OFFICIAL LOGO
==================================================

Keep the official logo colors.

On white/light backgrounds:

- “Sabi” stays dark/black
- “Labs” stays Sabi green
- eye/S symbol stays Sabi green
- preserve original proportions

Do not recolor the logo blue.

Do not uppercase or recreate the logo as text.

==================================================
3. PRIMARY ACCENT
==================================================

Use Sabi green consistently for:

- primary CTA buttons
- active navigation state
- links
- small labels
- selected indicators
- form focus states
- subtle card highlights
- icon accents
- hover states

Do not overuse bright green.

The green should feel controlled and professional.

==================================================
4. NAVBAR
==================================================

Use a clean white or slightly translucent white navbar.

Suggested:

background:
rgba(255,255,255,0.85);

blur(14px);

1px solid var(--border);

Navigation text:
charcoal

Sabi green

Sabi green background

Official logo:
full-color original version

==================================================
5. HERO
==================================================

Keep the hero centered and simple.

Use:

Primary heading:
charcoal / near-black

Supporting text:
muted grey-green

Sabi green

background:
rgba(255,255,255,0.65);

1px solid rgba(40,100,70,0.10);

backdrop-filter:
blur(16px);

==================================================
6. TYPOGRAPHY
==================================================

Keep the typography mature and readable.

Use one main font system consistently.

If Maiandra GD is retained:
use it selectively for small brand accents, eyebrow labels, or short headings.

Do not let a decorative font dominate long body text or oversized hero headings if it reduces professionalism.

Use charcoal for major headings.

Use muted grey-green for supporting copy.

- green accent

- white surfaces

15. GLASSMORPHISM
==================================================

Keep glass effects subtle.

The white theme should primarily feel:

clean
corporate
premium
intelligent

Glass should add depth, not become the main visual style.

Use it sparingly for:
- navbar
- selected CTA block
- selected cards
- contact form

Avoid applying blur everywhere.

==================================================
16. REMOVE STRAY COLORS
==================================================

Audit the white theme for any leftover:

- blue
- cyan
- gold
- yellow
- orange
- purple

Replace these with:

- charcoal
- muted grey-green
- Sabi green
- white / off-white

Unless a color is needed for semantic status such as error/success/warning.

SABI GREEN
